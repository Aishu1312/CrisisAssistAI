[0:00 — HOOK]
During natural disasters or medical emergencies, every second counts. Standard web searches and chatbots often present walls of generic text or unverified, outdated information, which can be dangerous under stress.

[0:20 — WHAT IT IS]
CrisisAssist AI is an intelligent, multi-agent emergency response companion belonging to the "Agents for Good" track. It processes emergency queries in structured phases to triage priority, verify resources, and generate localized, life-saving advice.

[0:40 — SHOW THE BANNER]
(show assets/cover_page_banner.png)
As shown on the cover banner, CrisisAssist AI combines multi-agent triage, verified resources, and a robust security shield to offer a trustworthy emergency response assistant.

[1:00 — ARCHITECTURE]
(show assets/architecture_diagram.png)
Looking at our agent architecture diagram, the request enters through START into the SecurityCheckpoint node, which scrubs PII and checks for prompt injections. From there, the orchestrator_agent coordinates the pipeline, invoking the planner_agent to triage urgency levels and the worker_agent to fetch local relief centers via the MCP server. If a CRITICAL priority is triaged, the request pauses at the human_dispatch_gate for dispatch approval. Finally, the evaluator_agent reviews all recommendations before formatting them at the final_output node.

[3:00 — SECURITY & MCP]
To protect user privacy, the Security Checkpoint scrubs emails and phone numbers while keeping audit logs. The MCP Server runs dynamically in the background, exposing local disaster, shelter status, and emergency contacts databases to our worker agent.
